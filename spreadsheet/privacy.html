Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

At NxtStockStore, we prioritize the privacy and security of our visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit or make a purchase from our store.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete a purchase, initiate checkout, or contact us, we collect certain personal information, including:

  • Contact details: Your email address and phone number (to send download files and contact you regarding orders).
  • Order metadata: Package selections, add-ons, paid totals, and transaction timestamps.
  • Transaction IDs: Payment reference codes generated by Razorpay (we do not capture, process, or store your credit card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank passwords; these are handled entirely on Razorpay's secure payment gateway).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to execute your orders and run support services, specifically to:

  • Send purchase emails, transaction receipts, and digital spreadsheet files.
  • Respond to customer support tickets, questions, and inquiries.
  • Validate payments and track purchase history for refund claims.

3. Sharing and Disclosing Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business:

  • Razorpay: To verify payments and facilitate transactions.
  • Google Sheets & Web Apps: To record order details, automate buyer delivery emails, and organize customer data.

4. Data Retention

When you place an order through our website, we will maintain your order information (email, phone, transaction ID) in our Google Sheets record for administrative history and support purposes, unless and until you ask us to delete this information.

5. Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All data sent to our Apps Script endpoint is transferred securely over HTTPS.

6. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational procedures or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an adjusted "Last Updated" timestamp.
