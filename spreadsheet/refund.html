Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Thank you for purchasing our spreadsheet templates at NxtStockStore. Please read our policy regarding refunds and returns carefully.

1. Nature of Digital Products

All items sold on NxtStockStore are **digital files** (Google Sheets templates). Because digital products can be copied, saved, and instantly accessed upon receipt, they cannot be physically returned. Therefore, **all sales of digital templates are final and non-refundable** under ordinary circumstances.

2. Exceptional Circumstances

We want to ensure you have a positive experience. We will evaluate refund requests under the following special conditions:

  • Non-delivery of files: If you do not receive the email containing download links and access files within 24 hours of a validated payment, and our support team is unable to manually deliver them to you after being contacted, we will issue a full refund.
  • Corrupted or broken file links: If the provided templates are corrupted or structurally broken (e.g. formula calculation errors preventing normal function) and we are unable to resolve the error or provide a working version within 48 hours of reporting, you may request a refund.
  • Double purchase: If you accidentally purchased the same template tier twice in separate transactions, contact us within 24 hours of purchase with both payment reference IDs, and we will refund the duplicate charge.

3. Contacting Us for Support

Before submitting a refund request or filing a payment dispute with your bank, please write to us at hr@nxtstock.in. Almost all download or template issues can be resolved quickly by our support staff sending direct sheet shares or guiding you through usage steps.

When writing, please include:

  • Your purchase email address.
  • Your Razorpay Payment ID (e.g., pay_...).
  • A clear screenshot or description of the file issue you are encountering.

4. Refund Execution

Once a refund is approved by our management team, the transaction credit will be initiated back to your original payment method (Bank account, Credit Card, or UPI ID) through Razorpay. Refunds typically reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on bank processing schedules.
