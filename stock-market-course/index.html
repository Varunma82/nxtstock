COURSE OUTLINE

What You Will Learn

A structured, step-by-step curriculum designed to take you from absolute beginner to a profitable independent trader.

01

Module 1: Stock Market Basics

Develop a bulletproof foundation. Learn about exchanges (NSE/BSE), types of financial instruments, reading basic charts, and setting up a professional trading terminal.

  • Introduction to Trading vs. Investing
  • How Stock Market Exchanges Work
  • Sectors, Indexes, and Order Types
  • Setting Up Your Charting Setup (TradingView)
02

Module 2: Technical Analysis Masterclass

Learn how to read price action like a pro. Understand candlesticks, chart patterns, trends, volume analysis, and how to spot institutional buying zones.

  • Advanced Candlestick Patterns & Interpretations
  • Support, Resistance & Supply/Demand Zones
  • Key Indicators (RSI, MACD, EMAs) & Divergences
  • Chart Patterns (Head & Shoulders, Flags, Double Tops)
03

Module 3: Fundamental Analysis & Investing

Discover how to pick multi-bagger stocks for long-term wealth. Learn how to read balance sheets, P&L statements, financial ratios, and evaluate company management.

  • Reading Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
  • Key Financial Ratios (P/E, ROCE, Debt-to-Equity)
  • Sieving Value: Quantitative vs. Qualitative Analysis
  • Building a Long-term Passive Investment Portfolio
04

Module 4: Risk Management & Psychology

The difference between winners and losers. Master position sizing, stop-losses, trading journals, and developing the emotional control required to stay profitable.

  • The Mathematics of Drawdowns & Position Sizing
  • Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R) Optimization
  • How to Write and Maintain a Trading Journal
  • Overcoming FOMO, Greed, and Revenge Trading
